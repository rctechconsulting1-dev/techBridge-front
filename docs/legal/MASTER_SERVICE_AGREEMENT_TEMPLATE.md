# Master Service Agreement

**NOT FOR PUBLICATION.** This is a contract template for Enterprise and
other large/custom engagements, filled in and signed per deal — it is
deliberately not linked from the public site (see
`docs/guides/ENTERPRISE_LEAD_PLAYBOOK.md`). Before sending this to a real
counterparty, have a licensed attorney review it — this draft is grounded
in the same terms already live on `/terms-of-service` and
`/subscription-terms`, but an MSA is a signed contract with real
consequences and should get a proper review pass, especially the
liability cap and indemnification sections, before it's used.

Fill in every `[BRACKETED]` field before sending. Delete this notice
block once reviewed.

---

---

### 1. Services and Statements of Work

1.1. RD TechBridge will provide website development, system integration,
workflow and business process automation, AI agent implementation,
digital advertising management, and related technology consulting
services (the "Services") as described in one or more Statements of Work
("SOW") executed under this Agreement. Each SOW will reference this
Agreement and describe the specific scope, deliverables, timeline, and
fees for that engagement.

1.2. In the event of a conflict between this Agreement and a signed SOW,
the SOW controls with respect to the matters it specifically addresses;
this Agreement controls for all other matters.

---

### 2. Term

2.1. This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until
terminated as provided in Section 11, or until **[INITIAL TERM LENGTH,
e.g. "12 months"]** has elapsed, whichever is later, and then
automatically renews for successive **[RENEWAL TERM LENGTH, e.g. "12-month"]**
terms unless either Party gives at least **[NOTICE PERIOD, e.g. "60 days"]**
written notice of non-renewal before the end of the then-current term.

2.2. Individual SOWs may specify their own term, which governs that
specific engagement.

---

### 3. Fees and Payment

3.1. Fees for Services are as set out in the applicable SOW or order
form. Unless otherwise stated, invoices are due within **[PAYMENT TERMS,
e.g. "30 days"]** of issuance.

3.2. Late payments may accrue interest at 1.5% per month or the maximum
rate permitted by law, whichever is lower. RD TechBridge may pause work
on any engagement with an invoice more than 15 days past due, after
providing notice.

3.3. If Client has also purchased a recurring Plan subscription, billing
for that Plan is governed by RD TechBridge's
[Subscription Terms & Conditions](https://rctechbridge.com/subscription-terms),
which are incorporated into this Agreement by reference for that purpose.

3.4. All fees are exclusive of applicable taxes, which will be added
where required.

---

### 4. Intellectual Property

4.1. **Client Deliverables.** Upon receipt of full payment for a given
deliverable, Client owns the custom code, designs, and content created
specifically for Client's project under the applicable SOW, excluding
pre-existing or reusable materials described in Section 4.2.

4.2. **RD TechBridge Tools.** RD TechBridge retains ownership of any
proprietary frameworks, templates, internal tooling, AI agent
configurations, or other reusable components developed prior to or
independent of this engagement, even if incorporated into Client's
project. RD TechBridge grants Client a non-exclusive, royalty-free
license to use such components as part of the delivered project for as
long as Client remains a client in good standing, or as otherwise agreed
in the applicable SOW.

4.3. **Third-Party and Open-Source Software.** Any open-source or
third-party software used in Client's project remains subject to its own
license terms.

---

### 5. Confidentiality

5.1. Each Party agrees to keep confidential any proprietary information,
business data, credentials, or trade secrets disclosed by the other
Party during the term of this Agreement ("Confidential Information"), and
to use such information only as necessary to perform its obligations
under this Agreement.

5.2. Confidential Information does not include information that is or
becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving Party, was
already known to the receiving Party without an obligation of
confidentiality, or is independently developed without use of the
disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

5.3. This Section survives termination of this Agreement for **[SURVIVAL
PERIOD, e.g. "3 years," or "indefinitely" for trade secrets]**.

8.1. NEITHER PARTY WILL BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL,
CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR ANY LOSS OF PROFITS, REVENUE,
DATA, OR BUSINESS OPPORTUNITY, ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS
AGREEMENT.

8.2. EACH PARTY'S TOTAL AGGREGATE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO

8.3. The limitations in this Section do not apply to: (a) either Party's

### 9. Indemnification

9.1. **By Client.** Client will indemnify, defend, and hold RD TechBridge
harmless from any third-party claims, damages, or expenses (including
reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from: Client's breach of this
Agreement; content or materials Client provides; or Client's violation of
any law or third-party right.

9.2. **By RD TechBridge.** RD TechBridge will indemnify, defend, and hold
Client harmless from any third-party claims, damages, or expenses
(including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from a claim that the
deliverables, as delivered by RD TechBridge and used as intended, infringe
a third party's intellectual property rights — except to the extent the
claim arises from Client-provided content, Client's modifications, or
Client's use in combination with materials not provided by RD TechBridge.

Delivering the Services may require access to Client's hosting, domain
registrar, advertising accounts, or other third-party accounts. Client is
responsible for the accuracy of any credentials or permissions it
provides and for ensuring it has the authority to grant such access. RD
TechBridge is not responsible for actions taken by third-party platforms
(including account suspension, policy changes, or ad disapprovals) that
are outside its control.

### 11. Termination

11.1. Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience with
**[NOTICE PERIOD, e.g. "60 days"]** written notice, subject to any
minimum commitment term in an active SOW.

11.2. Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written
notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to
cure that breach within 30 days of receiving notice of it.

11.3. RD TechBridge may suspend or terminate Services immediately for
non-payment, in accordance with Section 3.2.

11.4. Work completed and expenses incurred up to the effective date of
termination are billable. Sections 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 12, and 13 survive
termination.

---

### 12. Dispute Resolution and Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California,
without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles. Any dispute arising
out of or relating to this Agreement will first be addressed through
good-faith negotiation between the Parties' designated representatives.
If unresolved within 30 days, the dispute will be submitted to binding
arbitration in California under the rules of the American Arbitration
Association, except that either Party may seek injunctive relief in
court to protect confidential information or intellectual property.
Venue for any court proceeding permitted under this Agreement is the
state or federal courts located in California.

---

### 13. General Provisions

13.1. **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, together with all SOWs and
the Data Processing Agreement referenced in Section 6, constitutes the
entire agreement between the Parties regarding the Services, and
supersedes all prior discussions or agreements not reflected here.

13.2. **Amendment.** This Agreement may only be amended in writing signed
by both Parties, except that RD TechBridge's Data Processing Agreement
and Subscription Terms may be updated as described in those documents.

13.3. **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the
other's written consent, except to a successor in connection with a
merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all assets.

13.4. **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is found
unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full effect.

13.5. **Notices.** Notices under this Agreement must be sent in writing
to the addresses set out above, or to such other address as a Party
designates in writing.
